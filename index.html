१ The studio

Feelings is a décor studio in Pune led by designer Mrunall Gokhale. We work in marigold and mogra, brass and banana leaf, silk and candlelight, and we care most about the part guests can't name: the way a room makes them feel when they walk in.

From an intimate Dohale Jevan at home to a full ballroom wedding, every setup is drawn, sourced and built for one family and one date. Nothing comes out of a catalogue.

Under the Banyan

Wedding · Samavasaran, Bhandarkar Institute, Pune

A hundred-year-old banyan already had the drama. We hung strands of mogra and marigold through its branches so the tree itself became the mandap, and lit it low enough that the evening stayed soft.

Rooted in Gold

South Indian ceremony · Temple styling

Jasmine strands, brass samai lamps, a carved jhula and rosewood. A setup that borrows from the temple courtyard without becoming a costume.

The Red Mandap

Wedding · Hotel ballroom, Pune

Carved pillars, a canopy of red roses and marigold, and a carpet aisle lined edge to edge with flowers, built on site by our crew.

Pomegranate

Baby shower · Dohale Jevan

The pomegranate stands for abundance and new life, so it became the whole palette: split fruit on brass urlis, amaranth spilling to the floor, and pomegranates hung through the trees.

Shiv & Parvati

Seemantonnayanam · Mehendi

A hand-painted Shiv–Parvati mural, marigold columns and a Shivling made entirely of red roses. Devotional, but made for a family celebration.

Ivory

Reception · Ceremony hall

४ How we work

From first call
to the last petal.

01

We listen

A call, then a site visit. We walk the venue, ask about the families, the rituals you're keeping, the colours you can't stand, and what the budget really is.

02

We design

Mood boards, palettes, flower lists and drawings for every function. You see the whole picture, and every rupee of it, before anything is ordered.

03

We build

Flowers bought fresh, frames built, strands tied by hand. Our crew sets up on site so the venue is ready before the first guest walks in.

04

We stay

We're on site through the event, fixing what wilts and moving what's in the way, so your family can stay in the moment instead of managing vendors.
